HYZEN67 exterior model

The Blender scene and generation scripts in models/ are an independently
constructed exterior study based on Lofree's published product photographs.
They are not manufacturer CAD and are not represented as an official Lofree asset.
Product design, Lofree marks and product photographs belong to their respective
rights holders. Downloaded modelling-reference photographs are not redistributed.

Primary reference: https://lofree.co.jp/products/hyzen-gaming-keyboard
Reference image URLs: models/references/sources.json

The English Lofree marking is the original official logo already included in
source/web/lofree-logo.png. See the existing Lofree asset notice and REFERENCE.md.
The original official driver product image, source/web/hyzen-product.webp, is
also used unchanged as a shared roof-color texture. See Product-image.txt.
Packed font outlines use the bundled Manrope and Pretendard fonts. Their license
notices are included alongside this file.

The app renders the exported GLB locally using Three.js 0.187.0, under the
MIT license in Three.js-MIT.txt. Studio lighting uses the CC0 HDRI described
in Studio-HDRI-CC0.txt. No hosted viewer or runtime CDN is required.
